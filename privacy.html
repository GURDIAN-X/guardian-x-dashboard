Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Guardian-X Digital Solutions ("Guardian-X", "we") is based in Dodoma, Tanzania. This policy explains what personal information we handle on gurdianx.com and in Prompt Forge, and why.

1. Information we handle

  • Account details: your email address, a name you choose, and your password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider; we do not read them. New passwords are checked against lists of known leaked passwords without the password itself leaving your device.
  • Verification: a short code sent to your email to confirm it is you.
  • What you write in Prompt Forge: the ideas, answers and files you submit so that a prompt can be prepared for you.
  • Usage information: basic technical and usage information needed to apply plan limits and keep the service running.
  • Messages to us: anything you send by email or WhatsApp, including your contact details.
  • On this website: your chosen language and, if you sign in, your sign-in session are kept in your browser.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To prepare prompts and apply the limits of your plan.
  • To reply to enquiries and to arrange custom work or payments.
  • To protect the service against misuse and to fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Service providers who help us

  • Sign-in and data hosting: Supabase.
  • AI providers: to prepare a prompt, the text you submit is sent to AI services such as Claude, OpenAI, Grok or Gemini, depending on your plan and availability. When current information is needed, Prompt Forge may search the web. Their own privacy terms apply to what they receive.
  • Payments: mobile-money providers (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Mixx by Yas, Halopesa, T-Pesa) and PayPal handle payments under their own terms.
  • Website resources: this page loads fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from unpkg and jsDelivr, so those services can see your IP address when the page loads.

4. Keeping information safe

AI keys are kept on our servers, not on your computer. We limit who can reach account data and we review our systems for security problems. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

5. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to close your account, by writing to info@gurdianx.com. We take into account Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022. You can also clear your browser storage at any time to remove the language and session data kept on this website.

6. Keeping information

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal duties and resolve disputes, then delete or anonymise it.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Guardian-X Digital Solutions, Dodoma, Tanzania. Email: info@gurdianx.com. WhatsApp.
